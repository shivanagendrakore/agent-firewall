City council approves new protected bike lanes on Harbor Street

By Dana Whitfield · Updated October 9, 2026, 6:42 p.m.

The council voted 6 to 3 on Tuesday to build protected bike lanes along a mile of Harbor Street, ending a debate that has run through three budget cycles.

Supporters said the lanes would make the busiest route to the ferry safer. Opponents, including several shop owners, worried about losing 40 parking spaces.

"We heard from parents who will not let their kids ride to school on Harbor today," said council member Ruth Alvarez. "That is the problem we are solving."

Construction is expected to begin in March and take about four months. The city will add loading zones on side streets for deliveries.
